學生作品與專題Student Works & Projects

學以致用，
從課堂走向社會 Learning
by doing

經管系學生透過咖啡館經營、專題研究、社企合作與國際交流，將課堂所學應用於真實的商業情境。以下為本系學生的代表作品與學習活動。 Our students apply what they learn in class to real business settings: running a café, conducting capstone research, partnering with social enterprises, and learning across cultures. Here is a selection of their work.

實務經營Practicum

學生經營咖啡館Student-Run Café

每週五於知足二館（藝文中心）營業。學生從進貨、盤點、成本控制到行銷推廣，親身體驗創業與經營管理。Open every Friday at the Arts Center. Students handle purchasing, inventory, cost control, and promotion, gaining hands-on entrepreneurial experience.

印尼異國蔬食Indonesian Vegetarian Cuisine

印尼學生將家鄉美食轉化為美味蔬食，並運用網路行銷推廣家鄉傳統小吃。Indonesian students reimagined dishes from home as vegetarian cuisine and promoted them through online marketing.

國際交流Global

本國生與外籍生文化交流Cross-Cultural Exchange

於花蓮鯉魚潭參加獨木舟課程，並透過團體活動增進本國生與外籍生的情誼。不同年級、不同國籍的同學一起學習、互相交流。Local and international students kayak together at Liyu Lake in Hualien and build friendships through group activities. Students of different years and nationalities learn side by side.

大愛新聞：印尼新生來慈大學經管DaAi News: Indonesian Students Join the Department

慈濟大學迎接 829 位新生，其中 33 位來自印尼，到經管系學習農業經營與行銷。學校尊重不同的信仰與生活習慣，並做了貼心的規劃。Tzu Chi University welcomed 829 new students, 33 of them from Indonesia, who came to study agricultural management and marketing in our department. The university made thoughtful arrangements to respect their different faiths and customs.

02社企專題Social Enterprise

東部生活型態：社企聚落專題Eastern Taiwan lifestyles: social enterprise projects

學生走訪花東在地農業與社會企業，以影像紀錄他們的故事與經營模式（Agrihubbers 系列）。 Students visited local farms and social enterprises in eastern Taiwan and documented their stories and business models on film (the Agrihubbers series).
