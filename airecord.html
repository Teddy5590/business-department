AI 協作紀錄AI Collaboration Record

本頁記錄我在設計與發布本網站時，與 AI（Claude）合作的完整過程。以下列出我給 AI 的每一則提示（原文照錄），以及我使用該提示的原因、AI 的回應，和它如何影響我的設計決策。 This page documents how I worked with an AI assistant (Claude) to design and publish this website. It lists every prompt I gave the AI, word for word, along with why I used it, what the AI produced, and how that shaped my design decisions.

01提示紀錄Prompt Log

每一則提示與說明Every prompt, with notes

提示 1Prompt 1專案啟動Kick-off

提供作業說明，開始合作Sharing the brief and starting the project

提示（原文）Prompt (verbatim)
[Attached: Department Website Design Project brief and assessment criteria] Lets do this together
AI 回應AI response

AI 沒有直接產生網站，而是先問我三個問題：網站要介紹哪個系、想要的視覺風格，以及要逐步合作還是先產生完整草稿。Instead of generating a site right away, the AI asked me three questions: which department the site was for, which visual direction I wanted, and whether to work step by step or start from a full draft.

說明與反思Notes / reflection

我先把完整的作業說明交給 AI，讓它了解評分標準（index.html、studentworks.html、graph 資料夾、GitHub Pages 發布）。AI 先釐清需求再動手，讓後續的成果更符合我的期待。I gave the AI the full assignment brief so it understood the grading criteria (index.html, studentworks.html, the graph folder, and GitHub Pages publishing). Because it clarified my requirements before building anything, the results that followed matched what I needed.

提示 2Prompt 2需求設定Requirements

選擇系所、風格與合作方式Choosing the department, style and workflow

提示（我的選擇）Prompt (my selections)
Department: I'll give a URL Style: Classic academic Pace: Step by step
說明與反思Notes / reflection

我選擇提供本系官方網站的網址，讓內容以真實資料為基礎。我選擇「逐步進行」，這樣每完成一頁都能先檢查，再決定下一步，也方便記錄每一則提示。I chose to provide my department's official URL so the content would be based on real information. I chose to work step by step so I could review each page before deciding the next move, which also made it easier to record every prompt.

提示 3Prompt 3內容蒐集・首頁 v1Content · Homepage v1

擷取系所內容，產生第一版首頁Extracting department content for the first homepage

提示（原文）Prompt (verbatim)
https://mdm.tcu.edu.tw/p/412-1021-2.php, this is the department introduction, and it must have a hyperlink to the https://mdm.tcu.edu.tw/p/412-1021-3692.php?Lang=zh-tw
AI 回應AI response

AI 讀取了系所簡介與學習成果兩個頁面，產生第一版 index.html：深藍與酒紅的經典學院風格、內部 CSS、教育目標、六間教學設備，並在主視覺、學生作品區與頁尾加入學習成果連結。所有照片與影片位置都先以標示好檔名的預留框呈現（例如 graph/hero-campus.jpg）。The AI read both the department introduction and the learning outcomes pages and produced index.html v1: a classic navy-and-maroon academic style with internal CSS, the educational goals, six teaching facilities, and the required learning outcomes link in the hero, student works section and footer. Every photo and video position was a labelled placeholder with its future file name (for example graph/hero-campus.jpg).

說明與反思Notes / reflection
  • 原網站的系所沿革是以圖片呈現，AI 無法擷取文字，因此簡介文字是 AI 依據教育目標與關鍵字撰寫，我需要自行確認內容是否正確。The original site shows the department history as images, so the AI could not extract that text. The introduction was written from the department's goals and keywords, so I needed to check its accuracy myself.
  • AI 沒有直接使用官網的照片，因為作業規定所有圖片必須放在自己的 graph 資料夾，而且官網照片有著作權。The AI did not reuse photos from the official site, because the assignment requires all images to be in my own graph folder and those photos are copyrighted.
提示 4Prompt 4雙語・重新設計Bilingual · Restyle

加入中英切換，並改為 MIT 風格Adding a language switch and an MIT-inspired restyle

提示（原文）Prompt (verbatim)
the page mustbe double launguage, english and chinese, or be able to swithc between langauges, and Could you please restyle the HTML file I just generated? I would like it to have a professional, academic look—reminiscent of a top-tier American university, specifically MIT—or perhaps a clean, minimalist aesthetic typical of American design-related academic programs.
AI 回應AI response

AI 重新設計首頁：單一強調色（紅色 #A31F34）搭配黑、白、灰，大型粗體無襯線字、細線分隔與編號區塊（01–05）。右上角加入「中文／EN」切換按鈕，會切換全頁文字並記住使用者的選擇。AI 也用螢幕截圖檢查桌機與手機版面，發現並修正了兩個 CSS 錯誤。The AI redesigned the homepage with one accent colour (red, #A31F34) plus black, white and grey, large bold sans-serif type, thin rules and numbered sections (01–05). It added a 中文 / EN switch in the header that changes all text on the page and remembers the visitor's choice. The AI also checked desktop and phone screenshots, and found and fixed two CSS bugs.

說明與反思Notes / reflection

本系有許多國際學生（例如印尼專班），所以網站需要中英雙語。我參考作業範例 2 的提示，但指定 MIT 作為參考，因為我想要乾淨、現代、有理工學術感的設計，而不是傳統的襯線字風格。The department has many international students (such as the Indonesian program), so the site needed to be bilingual. I adapted the wording of Example 2 from the assignment but named MIT specifically, because I wanted a clean, modern and rigorous look rather than a traditional serif style.

提示 5Prompt 5學生作品頁Student Works page

建立 studentworks.htmlBuilding studentworks.html

提示（原文）Prompt (verbatim)
its okay, lets go to the nextstep
AI 回應AI response

AI 依照官網學習成果頁的真實內容，以相同的設計與語言切換建立學生作品頁：九件精選作品（含分類篩選按鈕）、五支 Agrihubbers 社企專題影片、預留的作品影片與六格活動相簿。AI 也實際測試兩頁之間的連結、語言設定是否延續到下一頁，以及手機版面。Using the real content of the official learning outcomes page, the AI built the student works page with the same design and language switch: nine selected works with category filter buttons, five Agrihubbers social enterprise films, a reserved showcase video slot and a six-photo gallery. It also tested the links between the two pages, whether the language choice carried over, and the phone layout.

說明與反思Notes / reflection

我對第二版首頁感到滿意，所以直接進入下一步。分類篩選讓訪客能快速找到「專題研究」、「實務經營」、「國際交流」或「影音作品」。I was happy with homepage v2, so I moved straight on. The category filters let visitors quickly find projects, practicum, global or media work.

提示 6Prompt 6發布準備Publishing prep

準備發布到 GitHub PagesPreparing to publish on GitHub Pages

提示（原文）Prompt (verbatim)
Teddy5590
AI 回應AI response

我提供 GitHub 帳號後，AI 將網站打包成壓縮檔，並列出發布步驟：建立公開儲存庫、上傳檔案、在 Settings → Pages 選擇 main 分支。AI 提醒我：GitHub 無法上傳空資料夾，所以在 graph 資料夾中放了一個說明檔；index.html 必須放在儲存庫最上層，否則會出現 404。After I gave my GitHub username, the AI zipped the website and listed the publishing steps: create a public repository, upload the files, and choose the main branch under Settings → Pages. It warned me that GitHub cannot upload an empty folder, so it added a small README to the graph folder, and that index.html must sit at the top level of the repository to avoid a 404.

03我的決策My Decisions

我自己做的設計決定Design decisions I made

  • 內容來源Content source使用本系官方網站作為內容基礎，並指定必須連結學習成果頁。Using the official department site as the content source, and requiring a link to the learning outcomes page.
  • 雙語網站Bilingual site要求中英文切換，服務本系的國際學生與訪客。Requiring a Chinese/English switch to serve international students and visitors.
  • 視覺方向Visual direction從經典學院風改為 MIT 啟發的極簡風格。Moving from a classic academic style to an MIT-inspired minimalist one.
  • 發布方式Publishing將儲存庫命名為 business-department，並透過 GitHub Pages 公開發布。Naming the repository business-department and publishing it publicly with GitHub Pages.
04總結反思Final Reflection

我從這次合作中學到什麼What I learned from working with AI

AI 幫助我快速完成網站的結構、程式碼與雙語內容，也協助我在發布前測試連結與手機版面。它在開始前會先確認需求，並在發現錯誤時主動修正，讓整個過程比我自己從頭寫程式更有效率。AI helped me build the site's structure, code and bilingual content quickly, and it tested the links and phone layout before publishing. It asked about my requirements before starting and fixed bugs it found, which made the process much faster than writing everything from scratch.

不過，網站的方向仍是由我決定：要介紹哪個系、要放哪些內容、要什麼風格，以及是否需要雙語。我也了解到 AI 撰寫的內容必須經過人工確認，因為它無法讀取圖片中的文字，有些內容需要我依實際情況修正。The direction of the site, though, was mine: which department to present, what content to include, which style to use, and whether it should be bilingual. I also learned that AI-written content must be checked by a person. The AI could not read text inside images, so some content needed me to verify it against reality.

最重要的是，我學會把需求說清楚：當我的提示越具體（例如指定 MIT 風格、指定必須連結的網址），AI 的成果就越符合我的期待。接下來的課程中，我會把自己拍攝的照片與影片放進 graph 資料夾，並持續記錄後續的提示。Most importantly, I learned to state my requirements clearly. The more specific my prompt (naming MIT as the style, giving the exact URL to link), the closer the result was to what I wanted. In upcoming classes I will add my own photos and videos to the graph folder and keep recording each new prompt here.
